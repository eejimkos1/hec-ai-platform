3. Feedstock Quality Prediction & Dynamic Pricing

ML classification of incoming petroleum waste upon arrival, predicting recovery value and setting dynamic acceptance pricing based on predicted oil yield, contaminant levels, and processing difficulty.
What They Need
Historical lab analysis data (viscosity, density, water/solids content), reception logs with pricing, processing outcomes per batch. Near-infrared (NIR) or inline sensors for real-time intake characterization.
How to Implement
Train gradient-boosted classifiers on lab data to predict waste category and expected yield within 15 minutes of intake sampling. Build a pricing engine that adjusts acceptance fees based on predicted processing cost and recovery value. Deploy as tablet app at reception docks.
What They Will Achieve
Eliminate unprofitable waste batches (currently ~8% of intake), reduce lab turnaround from 4 hours to 15 minutes, increase average margin per batch by 12-18% through dynamic pricing aligned to actual recovery economics.
Estimated Annual Impact
€2.5M — €4.0M

4. Multi-Jurisdictional Regulatory Compliance

GenAI-powered RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system for automated MARPOL compliance reporting across Greece, Germany, Gibraltar, and Malta — each with different waste declaration requirements.
What They Need
MARPOL Annex I/II/VI regulations, EU Ship Recycling Directive, national port state control requirements (4+ jurisdictions), historical compliance filings, waste delivery receipts (BDN documents), crew reporting templates.
How to Implement
Build a RAG system indexing all regulatory documents (MARPOL, EU directives, national rules). Auto-generate port-specific waste delivery reports. LLM-assisted anomaly detection on declarations vs actual quantities. Automated translation for multi-language filings.
What They Will Achieve
Reduce compliance team workload by 60%, eliminate filing errors (currently 3-5 per month leading to delays/fines), ensure 100% regulatory coverage across all operating jurisdictions, cut average filing time from 4 hours to 30 minutes.
Estimated Annual Impact
€800K — €1.5M (+ risk avoidance)
🔧

5. Predictive Maintenance for Plants & Fleet

Sensor-based ML for predictive maintenance of centrifugal separators, pumps, heat exchangers at processing plants, and main engines/auxiliaries on the 25-vessel collection fleet — all handling corrosive petroleum waste cargo.
What They Need
Vibration sensors on centrifuges and rotating equipment, temperature/pressure logs from PLCs, engine room monitoring data (cylinder pressures, lube oil analysis), maintenance work order history (CMMS), spare parts inventory and lead times.
How to Implement
Deploy IoT vibration/temperature sensors on critical equipment. Train anomaly detection models (Isolation Forest, LSTM autoencoders) on normal operating patterns. Build remaining-useful-life (RUL) estimators for centrifuge bearings and vessel engines. Integrate with maintenance scheduling for condition-based rather than time-based PM.
What They Will Achieve
Reduce unplanned downtime by 40-60% (centrifuge failures currently cost €50K+ per incident in lost production), extend equipment life by 15-25%, optimize spare parts inventory (reduce carrying cost by 20%), prevent catastrophic failures in corrosive environments.
Estimated Annual Impact
€3.0M — €5.0M
🤖

6. GenAI Customer & Market Intelligence

AI-powered market scanner for port/rig expansion opportunities, automated RFP response generation, customer churn prediction, and competitive intelligence across the Mediterranean waste management market.
What They Need
CRM data (customer contracts, volumes, pricing history), port authority tender notices, AIS vessel tracking data for competitor fleet analysis, shipping market reports, historical RFP submissions and win/loss records.
How to Implement
Build web scrapers for port authority tenders and shipping news. Train churn prediction model on customer behavior patterns. Deploy GenAI for automated RFP drafting (using RAG over past winning proposals). Create market intelligence dashboard showing competitor movements and expansion opportunities.
What They Will Achieve
Identify 3-5 new port expansion opportunities per year, increase RFP win rate from ~25% to ~40%, reduce customer churn by 15-20% through early intervention, cut RFP response time from 2 weeks to 3 days.
Estimated Annual Impact
€1.5M — €3.0M (revenue growth)

7. Carbon Footprint & ESG Analytics

AI-powered Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), automated ESG reporting under EU CSRD, and carbon credit modeling for waste-to-fuel operations under the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS) extending to maritime in 2024+.
What They Need
Energy consumption data per process stage, fuel consumption records (fleet + plant), waste input/output mass balances, emission factors database, EU ETS verification methodology, GHG Protocol Scope 1/2/3 boundaries, CSRD reporting templates.
How to Implement
Build automated LCA pipeline calculating CO2-equivalent per tonne of waste processed vs alternative disposal methods. Deploy GenAI for CSRD-compliant narrative generation. Model carbon credit eligibility for recovered fuel displacing virgin fossil fuels. Create real-time ESG dashboard for investor/regulatory reporting.
What They Will Achieve
Monetize carbon credits (€15-30 per tonne of CO2 avoided), achieve CSRD compliance ahead of 2025 deadline, attract ESG-focused investors with quantified environmental impact, position HEC as sustainability leader in maritime waste management.
Estimated Annual Impact
€1.0M — €2.5M (+ strategic positioning)
