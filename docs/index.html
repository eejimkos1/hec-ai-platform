Future AI Topics

5 additional ML/GenAI use cases for the next phase of HEC's transformation

5
Use Cases
03

Feedstock Quality Prediction & Dynamic Pricing

ML classification of incoming petroleum waste, predicting recovery value and setting dynamic acceptance pricing based on predicted oil yield and contaminant levels.
Requirements
Historical lab analysis data (viscosity, density, water/solids content), reception logs with pricing, processing outcomes. NIR or inline sensors for real-time intake characterization.
Implementation
Gradient-boosted classifiers on lab data to predict waste category and yield within 15 min. Pricing engine adjusting acceptance fees by predicted processing cost. Tablet app at reception docks.
Business Outcome
Eliminate unprofitable batches (~8% of intake), reduce lab turnaround from 4 hrs to 15 min, increase margin per batch by 12-18%.
Annual Impact€2.5M — €4.0M
04

Multi-Jurisdictional Regulatory Compliance

GenAI RAG system for automated MARPOL compliance reporting across Greece, Germany, Gibraltar, and Malta — each with different waste declaration requirements.
Requirements
MARPOL Annex I/II/VI regulations, EU Ship Recycling Directive, national port state control requirements (4+ jurisdictions), historical compliance filings, BDN documents.
Implementation
RAG system indexing regulatory documents. Auto-generate port-specific waste delivery reports. LLM anomaly detection on declarations. Automated translation for multi-language filings.
Business Outcome
Reduce compliance workload by 60%, eliminate filing errors (3-5/month), 100% regulatory coverage, cut filing time from 4 hours to 30 minutes.
Annual Impact€800K — €1.5M
05

Predictive Maintenance for Plants & Fleet

Sensor-based ML for predictive maintenance of centrifugal separators, pumps, heat exchangers, and vessel engines — all handling corrosive petroleum waste cargo.
Requirements
Vibration sensors on rotating equipment, temperature/pressure logs from PLCs, engine room monitoring data, maintenance work order history (CMMS), spare parts inventory.
Implementation
IoT sensors on critical equipment. Anomaly detection (Isolation Forest, LSTM autoencoders). Remaining-useful-life estimators for bearings and engines. Condition-based maintenance scheduling.
Business Outcome
Reduce unplanned downtime 40-60%, extend equipment life 15-25%, optimize spare parts inventory (-20% carrying cost), prevent catastrophic failures.
Annual Impact€3.0M — €5.0M
06

GenAI Customer & Market Intelligence

AI-powered market scanner for port expansion opportunities, automated RFP generation, customer churn prediction, and competitive intelligence across Mediterranean markets.
Requirements
CRM data (contracts, volumes, pricing), port authority tender notices, AIS vessel tracking for competitor analysis, shipping market reports, historical RFP win/loss records.
Implementation
Web scrapers for tenders and shipping news. Churn prediction model. GenAI for RFP drafting (RAG over winning proposals). Market intelligence dashboard for competitor movements.
Business Outcome
3-5 new port opportunities/year, RFP win rate 25% to 40%, reduce churn 15-20%, cut RFP response from 2 weeks to 3 days.
Annual Impact€1.5M — €3.0M
07

Carbon Footprint & ESG Analytics

AI-powered LCA, automated ESG reporting under EU CSRD, and carbon credit modeling for waste-to-fuel operations under the EU ETS maritime extension.
Requirements
Energy consumption per process stage, fuel records (fleet + plant), waste mass balances, emission factors, EU ETS methodology, GHG Protocol Scope 1/2/3, CSRD reporting templates.
Implementation
Automated LCA pipeline (CO2-eq per tonne processed). GenAI for CSRD narrative generation. Carbon credit modeling for recovered fuel displacing fossil fuels. Real-time ESG dashboard.
Business Outcome
Monetize carbon credits (€15-30/tCO2 avoided), CSRD compliance, attract ESG investors, position as sustainability leader in maritime waste management.
Annual Impact€1.0M — €2.5M
